# Bannerforge privacy policy

*Last updated: 19 October 2026*

Bannerforge is the launcher for the games Oathfront and Legion Extreme. This policy explains what information the
launcher and these games use, and why.

## Accounts

A Bannerforge account is a username and a password. No e-mail address or other personal details are asked for.

- When you create an account or sign in, your username and password are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS)
  connection to **Unity Authentication**, a service of Unity Technologies that stores the account for us.
  Unity's handling of this data is described in the
  [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy).
- If you choose *Keep me signed in*, your username and password are saved on your PC only, encrypted with
  Windows so that only your Windows user account can read them. *Sign out* deletes them.
- You can use Bannerforge without an account by choosing *Continue offline*.

## Playing online

When you start a game while signed in, the launcher passes your sign-in session to that game so it can use Unity's
online services (Lobby and Relay) to let you play with friends. Other players in a game room see your username and
messages you send in the in-game chat.

## Downloads and updates

To install and update games, Bannerforge downloads update information and game files from GitHub
(github.com). Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address when files are downloaded; see the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## What we don't do

Bannerforge shows no advertising, contains no analytics or tracking, and we do not sell or share your information
with anyone other than the services named above, which are needed to run accounts, online play and downloads.

## Your data

Installed games and settings are stored on your PC and are removed when you uninstall Bannerforge (and the games).
To have your account deleted, or for any question about this policy, open an issue at
<https://github.com/Zerrrex/Oathfront-Playtest/issues> (mention your username; never post your password).

Bannerforge is not directed at children under 13.
